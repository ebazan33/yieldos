Common questions

Is there really a free tier, or is it a 7-day trial trick?

Real free tier. Up to 5 holdings, full paycheck calendar, full Path to FIRE, daily AI briefing. No credit card required to sign up. The paid tier (Grow, $9/month) unlocks unlimited holdings, portfolio snapshots, and CSV imports of any size. You can sit on the free tier indefinitely.

What brokerages can I import from?

Fidelity, Schwab, Vanguard, E*TRADE, TD Ameritrade, Robinhood, Questrade, and Wealthsimple via direct CSV import. We auto-detect symbol, shares, price, and cost basis columns across all of them. For brokerages without a CSV export, you can add holdings manually in about 10 seconds each.

How is the dividend data sourced?

Live data from Polygon.io (US tickers) for prices, yields, and dividend payment history. For Canadian and other international tickers, you provide the price on import; we handle FX conversion to USD using daily ECB reference rates so portfolio totals stay consistent.

Who's behind YieldOS?

Built and run by Elian Bazan, a solo developer. No VC, no investors, no plans to sell user data. Yieldos exists because every other dividend tracker treats income as a side-feature of portfolio balance. Built specifically for the FIRE community.

What happens if I cancel?

Your data stays in your account on the free tier indefinitely. Paid features (snapshots, unlimited holdings) lock when your subscription ends, but you keep all your holdings and history. Cancel anytime through Stripe's customer portal. No email-the-founder hoops.
